Problem & Solution

Mold starts when copra stays wet too long

Open sun and smoke drying depend on the weather

Rain or cloudy skies keep the coconut meat wet. Warm, wet copra is where the mold Aspergillus flavus grows.

Aflatoxin is hard to remove

It is a toxin made by mold. It resists heat and is hard to take out once it forms. Stopping it early is easier than fixing it later.

Dry it to 6% moisture within 48 hours

That is the goal CopraCure is built for. It measures moisture all the time and tells you when drying is falling behind.

Process Workflow

How CopraCure works

Sun first, heater when needed, and a camera that never gets tired.

1

Load the tray

Spread fresh coconut meat on the steel mesh tray. Close the door and lock both latches.

2

The chamber dries

The clear roof lets the sun warm the air. At night or in the rain, the heater takes over. Fans push damp air out.

3

Sensors and camera watch

A probe reads copra moisture. Another sensor reads air temperature and humidity. The camera checks for mold.

4

You get the news

Readings appear on the web dashboard. If drying falls behind or mold shows up, you get an alert.

What is inside

The full build list, grouped by the job each part does. Tap a group to open it.

Brain and sensors - Reads the copra and the air, then decides what to switch on.
+ -
  • ESP32 dev board + breakout shield Main controller (30-pin, Type-C). Switches the heater, fans and light.
  • ESP32-CAM with OV2640 camera 2 MP camera for watching mold. Comes with a Type-C programmer board.
  • SHT40 temperature and humidity probe Reads the air temperature and humidity inside the chamber.
  • Two-prong stainless-steel moisture probe Reads moisture in one representative piece of copra.
Heat, air and light - Dries the copra and lights it for the camera.
+ -
  • 600 W PTC ceramic air heater (220 V) Backup heat for night and rain.
  • 12 V fan on the heater fins Blows the heated air into the chamber.
  • Metal heater cage Non-combustible perforated guard so the heater is not close to the tray or insulation.
  • 2 exhaust fans, 120 mm, 12 V DC Push humid air out for forced airflow.
  • 2 lower air intake vents Adjustable louvers with an insect screen.
  • LED strip, 12 V, IP67, cool white Even light next to the camera.
  • 8 W UV germicidal lamp Cleans the chamber between batches.
Power and switching - Turns the controller's small signals into heat and airflow.
+ -
  • SSR-25DA (or 40DA) + heatsink + thermal paste Switches the 220 V heater and stays cool.
  • 12 V 5 A power supply Powers the fans and the LED strip.
  • LM2596 / XL4015 buck converter Steps 12 V down to 5 V for the ESP32 boards.
  • 3- or 4-channel MOSFET module Lets the controller switch the 12 V fans and LED.
  • Relay module + door limit switch Switches the UV lamp and shuts it off when the door opens.
Safety and wiring - Protects people and keeps the wiring neat.
+ -
  • IP65 waterproof ABS box with hinge 285 x 189 x 140 mm control box on the left side.
  • RCD/RCBO + 6 A/10 A fuse or MCB 30 mA leak protection and overload protection.
  • Main ON/OFF switch + red emergency cutoff Master switch (16 A) and a 22 mm mushroom button.
  • KSD301 85 °C thermostat + thermal fuse Over-heat protection that works on its own. Fuse rated 102 °C or 115 °C, 10 A.
  • 3-prong grounded cord, ring terminals Earths the exposed metal parts.
  • Terminal blocks, ferrules, cable glands, heat-shrink Tidy, secure, splash-safe connections.
  • Wire and boards AWG 14 or 16 heat-resistant wire for 220 V, AWG 20 to 22 for 12 V, perfboard and jumper wires.
Chamber body - Built from the engineering drawing: 24 x 24 x 30 in.
+ -
  • 1/2 in marine plywood Walls, door panel, door frame and floor.
  • 3 mm clear polycarbonate or acrylic The sloped solar roof.
  • PE foam insulation with foil Keeps heat in the walls and the door.
  • Plain GI sheet Heat shield on top of the plywood floor.
  • 304 stainless wire mesh (1/2 or 1/4 in holes) The copra tray, 16/18 gauge.
  • 1 x 1 in aluminum angle bar Tray frame and support rails.
  • 3 in stainless butt hinges, 2 toggle latches Door hinges and the latches that pull it shut.
  • EPDM weather strip Seals the door against rain and air leaks.
  • Screws, rivets, bolts, high-temp silicone Holds and seals everything together.
Checking the results - Tools to confirm the machine's readings are right.
+ -
  • 4-pin copra moisture meter Reference check for the 6% goal.
  • Digital thermometer-hygrometer Baseline for traditional sun drying.
  • Digital scale, 5 kg x 0.1 g Weighing method to double-check moisture.
  • Aflatoxin lateral flow rapid test kits Positive or negative result after drying.
  • Sample tubes, gloves, sterile bags For clean, safe sampling.

Built to be safe around heat and power

Power cuts on a leak or overload

RCD/RCBO (30 mA) with a fuse or breaker on the 220 V line.

Two over-heat guards

An 85 °C thermostat plus a separate thermal fuse, apart from the controller.

Big red emergency switch

One press cuts the power, along with a main on/off switch.

UV lamp off when the door opens

A door switch keeps the cleaning lamp from running with the door open.

Heater kept away from the copra

A metal cage keeps it clear of the tray and the insulation.

Sealed control box

Waterproof IP65 box, grounded metal parts, and cable glands on every wire entry.

How we will prove it works

CopraCure is a prototype that dries one batch on one tray. These are the checks planned before we claim anything.

Does it reach 6%?

Moisture reduction and drying rate over the 48-hour window.

Does the camera judge mold correctly?

Camera readings (None, Low, High) compared with the real condition, scored with a confusion matrix.

Is the chamber steady?

Temperature and humidity logs, measured by standard deviation.

Is the copra safe?

Aflatoxin lateral flow rapid test kits on the dried copra: positive or negative.

Can farmers use it?

Farmers and technical experts rate usefulness, reliability and ease of use (ISO/IEC 25010), with a survey in Tagalog.
